    How FeedSentinel works

    FeedSentinel watches several copies of the same market data arriving over different paths and flags any feed that looks garbled, delayed, duplicated, frozen or otherwise broken, in real time, with a plain-language explanation and a recommended action.

    Eight layers, cheapest first

    1. L0
      Market context: session calendar, system events and trading halts, so a quiet or halted market is never mistaken for a dead feed.
    2. L1
      Gatekeeper: every message is checked against the schema and sanity rules (price scale, tick size, crossed book, test symbols). Bad messages are quarantined, not silently dropped.
    3. L2
      Sequence & timing: MoldUDP64-style sequence tracking for gaps, duplicates, out-of-order delivery and resets; heartbeats for disconnects; latency and clock skew per feed.
    4. L3
      Feature windows: rolling per-feed windows of message rates, latencies, price-change and divergence statistics.
    5. L4
      AI: an Isolation Forest scores how unusual each window is against learned normal behaviour, and a fault classifier names the most likely fault.
    6. L5
      Trust-weighted consensus: feeds are compared as-of exchange time, so a feed that is alive but wrong (frozen, stale, drifting, spiking, decimal-shifted) stands out against its peers.
    7. L6
      Health & state machine: a 0–100 health score and HEALTHY / DEGRADED / CRITICAL state per feed. Escalation follows a corroboration rule, so a single weak signal on its own does not raise an alarm.
    8. L7
      Incidents & explanation: one incident per root cause with a runbook action and root-cause analysis, plus an explanation grounded only in the measured evidence (LLM-written when enabled, template otherwise).

    What is real, simulated and roadmap

    StatusWhat
    Real Nasdaq-derived order-book data (LOBSTER sample of Nasdaq TotalView-ITCH), the Nasdaq Symbol Directory, every detector (L0–L7), the trained ML models, the evaluation harness and this dashboard.
    Simulated The three feed paths (Direct, SIP, Vendor are split from one real source, each with its own latency profile) and the faults injected from the Chaos Lab.
    Roadmap A native ITCH 5.0 binary decoder, a live Nasdaq Cloud Data Service (NCDS, Kafka) connection, and alerting integrations (paging, chat, ticketing).

    Reading the Monitor

    • Feed cards: health 0–100, state (● HEALTHY, ⚠︎ DEGRADED, ✖︎ CRITICAL), throughput, latency, counters, AI anomaly score against its threshold, and the classifier’s diagnosis.
    • Price chart: consensus (thick line) against each feed; vertical lines mark incidents, coloured by severity.
    • TTD (time to detect) is measured in market seconds, so it does not depend on replay speed.
    • “Real market events” in the Chaos Lab are genuine market behaviour; the right answer is no alert.
